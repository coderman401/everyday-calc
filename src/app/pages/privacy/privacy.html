The short version

  • There is no sign-in, no account and no server that stores your calculations.
  • Analytics cookies are off until you accept them, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • Nothing you type into a calculator is sent to us or to Google.

What stays on your device

Every calculation is worked out by your browser. The numbers you enter are not uploaded and are forgotten when you close or refresh the page. Two small settings are remembered on your device: your cookie choice and, if you pick one, light or dark theme.

What analytics collects, if you accept

We use Google Analytics 4, run by Google, to learn which pages and calculators people use and whether the site is fast. Only after you press Accept analytics does your browser load Google’s script. It then reports:

Google and where data goes

Google processes analytics data on our behalf, on servers that may be outside India and your country of residence. Event data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics account, up to 14 months, and is then deleted. Google’s own terms are at policies.google.com.

Why we may do this, and your rights

Our basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time without giving a reason. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask what data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because analytics data is not tied to your name, we may need the identifier in your browser’s Google cookie to find it. Write to {{ contact }}.

Children

This site is for adults and is not aimed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this page

If what we collect changes, this page changes first and the cookie banner asks again. The date at the top shows the latest version.
